# Focus

Work with your agents without the rest of Buzz competing for attention.

## Getting started

1. Open the menu under your profile picture and choose **Focus**.
2. Pick how long, from 30 minutes to the rest of today or a custom end time,
   then choose **Start focus**. Or press ⌥F (Alt+F) to focus for an hour, and
   again to end it.
3. The sidebar now shows only your Solo chats, and only Solo chats in the
   selected community can show banners, play sounds or light the Dock dot. Search (⌘K) and links still open any
   channel or DM. Your status becomes 🔭 Focusing.
4. Focus ends by itself, or choose **End** in the sidebar. Your tabs and
   earlier status come back.

---

## Details

- Focus is saved on this device and survives a restart. The status is set in
  the selected community only, and is restored only if 🔭 is still your latest
  status there.
- If Focus ends while another community is selected, the earlier status comes
  back when you return to the first one. Starting Focus in another community
  before that replaces the saved status, so the first one is not restored.
- Alerts from everything else, including Reminders, are not shown or saved.
  Those conversations keep their unread state.
- Turning the plugin off mid-Focus shows everything again at once, and 🔭
  stays until it expires. Quitting Buzz keeps Focus on for next time.
- **Settings → Plugins → Focus** sets the default length: the one the dialog
  starts with and ⌥F uses. Change the shortcut's keys in
  **Settings → Shortcuts**.
