# Hide muted channels

Keep muted channels out of your sidebar until they need you.

## Getting started

1. Turn on **Hide muted channels** in **Settings → Plugins**.
2. Mute a channel from its row menu. It leaves its section and appears in a
   collapsible **Muted** section, where you can open it.
3. A muted channel comes back to its section while it has an unread mention,
   reply in your thread, broadcast or manual unread mark, and while it is open.
   Starred channels and direct messages always stay where they are.
