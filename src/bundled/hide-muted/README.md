# Hide muted channels

Keep muted channels out of your sidebar until they need you.

## Getting started

1. Turn on **Hide muted channels** in **Settings → Plugins**.
2. Mute a channel from its row menu. It leaves its section. To open it, use the
   section's **⋯ → Filter** menu, where it is listed under **Muted**, or search.
3. A muted channel comes back to its section, with a dot, while it has an unread
   mention, reply in your thread, broadcast or manual unread mark, and while it
   is open. Starred channels and direct messages always stay where they are.
