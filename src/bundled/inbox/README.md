# Inbox

Brings your mentions, direct messages, and thread replies together in one place, so you can catch up quickly.

## Getting started

1. Open **Inbox** in the sidebar.
2. Select a conversation to read and reply without leaving Inbox.
3. Use **Inbox filters** to focus on **Threads**, **Mentions**, or **DMs**, or to find your **Drafts**.

When someone tags you in a chat they shared with you, it also appears under **Inbox** in the sidebar until you have read it and moved on.
