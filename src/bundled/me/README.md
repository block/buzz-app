# Solo

Your personal space for chatting with your agents, organized your way.

## Getting started

1. Open **Solo** in the top bar and choose **New chat**, or press Cmd+N (Ctrl+N on Windows and Linux).
2. Open **Session settings…** to change a chat's name, workspace or lifetime. Use **Move to** to organize it in a project.
3. Choose **Share…** to manage access and create a link to a chat.
