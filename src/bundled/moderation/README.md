# Membership

Invite people to your community and manage their roles and access.

The plugin is on by default; **Settings → Plugins → Manage plugins** can turn it off.

Membership appears under **Community admin** in Settings when you select a
community where you are an owner or admin.

## Getting started

1. Go to **Settings → Community admin → Membership**.
2. Switch between **Members** and **Invite** in the same screen.
3. Use a member's menu to manage their role or remove access, according to your permissions.

Direct addition by public identity is retained behind `DIRECT_MEMBER_ADD_ENABLED`
for a possible return, but is hidden in the shipped UI.
