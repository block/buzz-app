# Recent Conversations

Puts your latest Solo chats and direct messages, and the Team channels where you were most recently mentioned or got a reply in a thread you follow, in one list, so you can work through them without searching the sidebar.

## Getting started

1. Find **Recent** at the top of the sidebar, above **Solo** and **Team**, in the **All** view. It shows your eight most recent conversations, read or unread; choose **Show more** for older ones.
2. Press **⌘D** (**Ctrl+D** elsewhere) to go down the list and **⌘U** (**Ctrl+U**) to go up. Both step through the eight conversations shown and wrap at the ends. Team channels open at the thread that put them there; Solo chats and DMs open the conversation.
3. Change the shortcuts in **Settings → Shortcuts**.
