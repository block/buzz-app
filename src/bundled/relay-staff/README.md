# Moderation

For authorized relay staff reviewing reports and feedback across communities on
the connected relay.
Community membership management belongs in **Membership**. Being a community
owner or admin does not grant relay staff access. Only operators can manage staff.

## Getting started

1. Open **Settings** while connected to the relay you administer.
2. Under **Moderation**, choose **Reports**, **Feedback**, or **Communities**. These entries appear only when the relay grants access.
3. Operators can also use **Staff** to manage relay staff. Use **Check again** to refresh your access.
