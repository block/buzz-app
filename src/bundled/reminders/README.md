# Reminders

Reminds you about your own text or a message at a time you choose.

## Getting started

1. Open **Reminders** in the app dock and choose **New reminder** (+) to write your own reminder. To start from a message, choose **Remind me** in its menu and add a note if you like.
2. Pick a time, like **In 1 hour** or **Tomorrow at 9am**, then choose **Set reminder**.
3. Open **Reminders** to see what's coming up, and **Snooze** or **Mark done** when you're ready.
4. To change what a pending reminder says, choose **Edit** in its **More actions** menu.

---

## Details

- A reminder you wrote opens **Reminders** in that community when you select its banner.
  A message reminder also opens **Reminders** if you no longer have access to its channel.
