# Voice Jump

Go to a conversation by saying its name.

## Getting started

1. Turn on a speech provider in **Settings → Speech to text**, and a
   decisions provider in **Settings → Decisions API**.
2. Press ⇧⌘J (Shift+Ctrl+J on Windows and Linux), or click the mic beside
   Search, and say where to go, such as "the design channel", "my DM with
   Alice" or "the thread about the launch".
3. The likeliest places show as you talk. When one is clearly it, Buzz opens
   it without waiting; otherwise pause, press Enter or pick a row.
4. Press ⇧⌘J again to stop or restart listening. You can also type instead.

---

## Details

- It chooses among the channels, DMs, agent chats and threads this device
  already has loaded, and never reads more from the community to do it.
- The speech provider you chose in Settings hears you, billed to the
  community you're in.
- The decisions provider gets what you said with the names, descriptions and
  a few recent messages of up to 200 places, and picks one. It opens a place
  at 50% or more, or at 80% while you're still talking.
